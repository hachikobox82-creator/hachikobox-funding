# HachikoBox – AI Character Marketplace & Ethical Ecosystem

**HachikoBox** ([hachikobox.com](https://hachikobox.com)) is an open, 100% live Progressive Web App (PWA) AI Character Marketplace built to empower creators, educators, businesses, and non-profits through interactive AI characters.

### Our Mission & Vision
Founded by Péter Szlavita and his daughter, HachikoBox combines a sustainable creator economy with social impact.
- **Phase 1 (Live):** An open AI Character Marketplace featuring instant personalization, creator monetization (subscriptions + ad engine), and transparent payouts.
- **Phase 2 (Roadmap):** Funding the development of an ethical food delivery platform where every order generates micro-donations for local animal shelters.

### Use of Funds
All contributions received via Drips directly support:
- Official company incorporation (Delaware C-Corp / EU entity) and payment gateway activation
- Google Play Store & Apple App Store native app publishing
- Scalable AI API and server infrastructure
